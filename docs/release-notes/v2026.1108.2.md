**Multiple calendar accounts and flexible sync.** Add multiple Google Calendar
and Outlook / Microsoft 365 accounts in Settings > Calendars. Each account
has separate encrypted credentials and independent Hide events, Pause sync,
Reconnect and Remove controls. Reconnecting keeps its visibility and sync
preferences. Existing connections remain usable after upgrading.

Choose Auto, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours or 1 day for calendar
sync. Auto checks sooner near meetings and backs off on quiet, unchanged days
and service failures. Refresh now remains available. Hiding events keeps the
connection and cached events, so showing them again needs no new sign-in.

Repeated sync runs only while Today is open. Lock, display-off and battery
saver pause it; reopening keeps a fresh interval and catches up when overdue.
No permanent calendar worker, new dependency or animation was added. Provider
consent and organization policies still apply; live sign-in needs user testing.
