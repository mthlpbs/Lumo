**Optional AI and connected calendars.** AI companion can now be switched on
or off in Add-ons. Switching it off hides its tab, shortcut and Settings
section while keeping its saved configuration and chats.

Today adds a seven-day read-only agenda with meeting links. Connect Google
Calendar or Outlook / Microsoft 365 in Settings > Calendars, or subscribe to
a custom ICS calendar. Microsoft calendar events include Teams join links.
The standalone Zoom account integration has been removed.

The Windows packaging script updates the date-based version across the app
and installers, then creates a standalone EXE, portable ZIP, setup installer
and checksums in a version-specific output directory. It tests the app and
verifies package contents before finishing. Unchanged rebuilds retain their
version. Local packaging does not create a tag or publish a release.

Calendar sync is read-only, runs on demand, and refreshes only while Today is
open. Lock, display-off and battery saver pause it. No permanent calendar
worker or new per-frame animation is added. Provider consent and tenant
policies still apply; live account sign-in requires user testing.
